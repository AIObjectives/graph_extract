"The Social Readjustment Rating Scale" (Journal of Psychosomatic Research, 1967).
Holmes, T. H., & Rahe, R. H.

holmes_rahe.csv: 43 life events with Life Change Unit (LCU) scores (11-100),
the classic Holmes-Rahe scale. Unsigned/valence-agnostic: mixes positive events
(e.g. marriage) and negative events (e.g. death of a spouse), scored purely on
magnitude of required life readjustment.
